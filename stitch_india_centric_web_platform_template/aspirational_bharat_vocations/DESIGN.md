## Brand & Style

This design system sets the standard for a premier, empowering vocational education and employment ecosystem crafted for India’s ambitious youth—bridging the gap between Tier-2 and Tier-3 talent and mainstream modern enterprise careers. 

The aesthetic avoids kitsch, cliches, and patronizing stereotypes. Instead, it balances institutional authority with cultural warmth through a **Modern Indian Tactile** design language:
- **Tone & Mood:** Confident, warm, dignified, and transformative. Every screen feels like an invitation into economic mobility and craft mastery.
- **Visual Character:** Crisp, hyper-legible typographic rhythms, clean editorial structure, and calibrated warmth. The interaction layer relies on fine 1px outlines, tactile surface lift, purposeful terracotta sparks, and verified emerald reassurance.
- **Accessibility & Context:** Optimized for mobile-first consumption across varying screen densities and intermittent network qualities, prioritizing immediate scanability of wages, credentials, skills outcomes, and localization hints without clutter.

## Colors

The palette balances institutional credibility with vibrant, grounded cultural accents:

- **Primary (`#1E2A4A` - Deep Royal Indigo):** Anchors navigation bars, primary headlines, structural frames, and primary action buttons. Evokes governance, high-tech standards, and unwavering security.
- **Secondary (`#D9532F` - Terracotta Clay):** Represents human industry, resilience, and ambition. Applied to focal calls to action, active milestone trackers, and aspirational pathway markers.
- **Accent Warmth (`#E67E22` - Kesari Amber):** Reserved for urgent admissions, scholarship tags, high-demand skill spotlights, and star ratings.
- **Tertiary (`#0F766E` - Forest Emerald):** Signifies tangible career outcomes, salary hikes, verified partner credentials (NSDC / Sector Skill Councils), and placement guarantees.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FAF8F5` - Pearl Chalk):** A warm off-white foundation that eliminates eye strain while offering warmer tactile contrast than clinical cold gray.
- **Surfaces (`#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity elevated cards, bottom sheets, and modal layers.
- **Text & Borders:** High-contrast Charcoal (`#0F172A`) for effortless legibility across light environments, muted slate (`#475569`) for metadata, and hairline warm-stone boundaries (`#E7E3DC`) to cleanly segment rich data.

## Typography

The type system prioritizes structural authority paired with effortless multi-script legibility:

- **Headline Font (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Selected for its modern geometric architecture, open apertures, and crisp letterforms. It carries optimism, strength, and executive presence on hero statements, course names, and institutional headers.
- **Body Font (`Inter`):** Delivers clean optical balance and neutral clarity across dense curriculum modules, placement criteria, and bilingual assistive glossaries.
- **Salary & Metric Rendering:** Figures featuring the Indian Rupee symbol (`₹`) and Lakh notations (`₹4.5L - ₹8.0L CTC`) leverage `label-salary` with tabular numbers (`tnum`) to maintain visual prominence and clean vertical alignment.
- **Bilingual Subtext:** Where Hindi or regional language cues accompany English titles, body sizing scales automatically down by 1px with a 500 (medium) weight to create clear hierarchy without visual noise.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a rhythmically fluid, mobile-first grid engineered to handle varying handheld viewport ratios:

- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `16px` (`1rem`) outer margins and `16px` gutters. Critical actions (apply, download syllabus, request callback) are anchored in fixed bottom action sheets.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with `24px` margins and gutters, supporting side-by-side trade comparisons and cohort timelines.
  - **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout max-width constrained to `1280px` with `40px` (`2.5rem`) outer margins and `24px` gutters.
- **Spacing Rhythm:** Standard 8pt / 4pt sub-grid system. Use `space-sm` (`8px`) for badge insets and related tag collections; use `space-md` (`16px`) for card internal paddings and standard stacked forms; use `space-xl` (`32px`) for sectional breaks between distinct career paths and hiring partner carousels.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through physical clarity, warmth, and deliberate contrast rather than heavy dramatic drop shadows:

- **Surface Base:** The `#FAF8F5` pearl ground creates a natural baseline. Layered content rests on clean `#FFFFFF` planes bordered with a delicate `1px solid #E7E3DC` warm-stone edge.
- **Elevation Level 1 (Resting Cards & Listings):** `0 1px 3px rgba(30, 42, 74, 0.04), 0 4px 12px rgba(30, 42, 74, 0.03)` with a `1px` border. This renders vocational courses, trade tracks, and partner logos sharp and distinct.
- **Elevation Level 2 (Interactive Hover & Elevated Badges):** `0 8px 24px rgba(30, 42, 74, 0.08), 0 2px 6px rgba(30, 42, 74, 0.04)`, slightly shifting the card upward by `-2px` on pointer hover.
- **Elevation Level 3 (Sticky CTAs & Dialog Overlays):** `0 16px 36px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`, grounding modal dialogs, verification certificates, and sticky mobile application bars.
- **Glass Accents:** Sticky navigation bars adopt a semi-translucent backdrop (`rgba(250, 248, 245, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) allowing scrolling colors to indicate continuous vertical motion.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a roundedness factor of `2` (8px standard corner radius, 16px for cards, and 24px for thematic containers):

- **Interactive Components (Buttons, Inputs, Selectors):** Standardized on `8px` (`0.5rem`) radius to evoke solid engineering and professional reliability.
- **Content Cards & Media Modules:** Standardized on `16px` (`1rem`) corner radius, creating a modern, friendly framing for workshop snapshots, student video testimonials, and job cards.
- **Pill Attributes (Badges, CTC Metrics, Location Tags):** Employ fully rounded pill geometries (`9999px`) to immediately communicate self-contained metadata at a glance.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Deep Indigo background (`#1E2A4A`), white text, `8px` radius, `12px 24px` padding. On focus/hover: subtle lighten to `#283863` with soft indigo shadow.
- **Aspirational / Apply CTA:** Terracotta background (`#D9532F`), white text, bold weight. Used strictly for "Apply for Cohort", "Book Career Counseling", or "Claim Scholarship".
- **Secondary / Outline:** Pure white background, `1.5px` border in Deep Indigo, dark slate text.
- **Bilingual Action Hint:** Secondary microtext directly below or inline with button labels (e.g., *Apply Now* / निशुल्क आवेदन करें).

### Cards
- **Course & Trade Card:** White surface, `16px` radius, `1px solid #E7E3DC`, containing category header, training mode tag (Online / Offline Center), job role title, salary bracket, hiring partner logos, and immediate action link.
- **Hiring Partner Card:** Crisp border, muted background on hover, featuring verified placement numbers (e.g., *340+ Placed in Tata Motors*).

### Badges & Micro-Pills
- **Salary Hike Badge:** Pill shape, soft mint-tinted green background (`#ECFDF5`), bold emerald green text (`#0F766E`), featuring rupee sign and hike metric (e.g., `₹4.2L - ₹6.5L CTC` or `+85% Hike`).
- **Certification & Affiliation Badge:** Soft indigo background (`#EEF2F6`), Deep Indigo text (`#1E2A4A`), prefixed with an official seal or shield icon (e.g., `NSDC Aligned` / `NASSCOM Verified`).
- **City Tag:** Warm gray background (`#F1EFEA`), muted slate charcoal text (`#334155`), displaying Tier-2/Tier-3 hubs (e.g., `Indore Hub`, `Lucknow Center`, `Coimbatore Campus`).

### Inputs & Form Fields
- Crisp `1px solid #CBD5E1` border with a `8px` radius and `#FFFFFF` background.
- Focus state: `2px` solid ring of Deep Indigo (`#1E2A4A`) with an inner inset white stroke.
- Explicit support for mobile OTP entry layouts, localized placeholder text, and one-tap Aadhaar/DigiLocker verification indicators.

### Lists & Curriculum Accordions
- Module headers feature step numerals bounded in neutral roundels, clear week-by-week delivery specs, and tangible job-readiness markers (e.g., "Solar Inverter Assembly & Maintenance Practical Lab").
- Checkmarks for completed modules use Emerald Green circles with crisp white glyphs.
